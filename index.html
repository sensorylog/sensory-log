AI Insights

Off by default. When turned on, a summary of your patterns — not your written notes, unless you choose to include them below — is sent to Claude to generate reflections. This needs an internet connection and only works while this page is open inside Claude.ai; it won't work in the version saved to your home screen.

Your data stays on this device

Nothing is uploaded unless you use AI Insights above. Export a backup so you don't lose history if you switch phones or clear browser data.

This is a personal tracking tool, not a diagnostic or medical device. Skip any day that feels like too much — there is no streak to protect. Pattern insights, trends and forecasts below are simple statistics computed only from your own entries; they're rough estimates from a small personal sample, not certainties or clinical conclusions.
