# Sensory Log

A calm, private tool for tracking energy, sensory load, masking, body signals, and what actually helps recovery.

Everything stays on your device. No account, no cloud, no tracking.

---

## Features

- **Energy** and **sensory saturation** scales
- **Masking** duration
- **Body signals** (tight chest, foggy, shutdown-y, etc.)
- **What drained you** (with custom tags)
- **Environment** (Home / Work / Public / Mixed)
- **Recovery needed**
- **What actually helped** — one of the most useful parts
- Automatic pattern insights after ~2 weeks of logging
- Dark mode
- Log or edit any past day
- Export as JSON or CSV
- Completely offline and private

---

## How to use

1. Open the live page:  
   **https://sensorylog.github.io/index.html
2. On iPhone: tap the Share button → **Add to Home Screen**  
   On Android: tap the menu → **Add to Home screen** / **Install app**

3. That’s it. It now works like an app and keeps your data on the device.

---

## Privacy

- All data is stored only in your browser’s local storage
- Nothing is ever uploaded to a server
- Clearing your browser data will erase your entries (so use the Export button for backups)
- Export JSON or CSV anytime

---

## Backups

Use the **Export JSON** or **Export CSV** buttons at the bottom of the page.  
You can import a backup later — it merges with existing entries so you won’t lose anything.

---

## Note

This is a personal tracking tool, not a diagnostic or medical device.  
Skip any day that feels like too much — there is no streak to protect.
